About

Engineer, team lead and entrepreneur

My journey in software development began in 2017 on the frontend, crafting interfaces with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript and connecting them to APIs. Over the years, that foundation matured into full-stack expertise: designing APIs, modelling data, building real-time features and delivering interfaces that remain fast and reliable under real-world demand.

I joined RwBuild, a Kigali-based startup, in November 2019 as a frontend developer and have since grown into a software engineer working across the full stack on mission-critical systems, most notably the dispatch platform behind Rwanda’s 912 emergency line. RwBuild’s annual workshop trainings have been central to that growth: I lead the frontend team through them, and mentoring others has refined my own engineering practice as much as theirs.

In July 2024 I founded Invaincu Ltd, a software company built on a single commitment: fast, robust software. Through a long-term partnership with RPC Ltd, Invaincu has rebuilt the complete digital products of two national newspapers: Imvaho Nshya is now live, and the new La Nouvelle Relève launches soon. Invaincu also developed BeautyHub, a salon booking and management platform available on Android, with iOS coming soon.
