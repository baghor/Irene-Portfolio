About

Engineer, team lead and founder

I have been building for the web since 2017. What began as frontend work grew into ownership of the whole product: designing APIs, modelling data, wiring real-time features and shipping interfaces that stay fast under real-world use.

I joined RwBuild, a Kigali-based startup, in November 2019 as a frontend developer. Over the years I grew into a software engineer, working across the stack on systems that cannot afford to fail, among them the dispatch platform behind Rwanda’s 912 emergency line. RwBuild’s yearly workshop trainings shaped much of that growth: I lead the frontend team through them, and guiding others has sharpened my own engineering as much as theirs.

In July 2024 I founded Invaincu Ltd, a software company built on one promise: fast, robust software. Under a working contract with Rwanda Printing Company (RPC), Invaincu is revamping the complete digital products of two national newspapers, Imvaho Nshya and La Nouvelle Relève. Invaincu is also behind BeautyHub, a salon booking and management platform with an Android app, and iOS coming soon.
